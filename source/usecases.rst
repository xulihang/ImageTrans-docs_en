Use Cases
==================================================

The earlier chapters of this manual introduced the features and operations of ImageTrans. While reading them, you may wonder more about "how exactly do I complete a specific task with ImageTrans." For that reason, the official blog of this site has published a series of scenario-based, step-by-step tutorials with screenshots. This page groups those tutorials by use case so you can quickly find the full workflow you need when it matters.


Manga Translation
--------------------------------

The overall workflow for translating comics and manga, from creating a project, drawing text regions, OCR recognition, machine translation, erasing the original text, to lettering and exporting the finished product.

* `Translating Japanese Manga with ImageTrans <https://www.basiccat.org/manga-translator/>`_
* `The Current Best Practice for Translating Japanese Manga <https://www.basiccat.org/best-practice-manga-ocr-and-translation/>`_
* `Lettering Guide for Japanese-English Manga Translation <https://www.basiccat.org/lettering-guide-for-Japanese-English-manga-translation/>`_
* `Screentone Preservation When Removing Text in Manga <https://www.basiccat.org/remove-text-in-manga-preserving-screentone/>`_
* `How to Translate Manga Purchased from DLsite <https://www.basiccat.org/how-to-translate-dlsite-manga/>`_
* `Translating American Comics with ImageTrans <https://www.basiccat.org/US-comics-translation/>`_
* `Translating Korean Webtoons with ImageTrans <https://www.basiccat.org/how-to-translate-raw-webtoon-in-korean/>`_
* `How to Convert Traditional Comics into a Webtoon Strip <https://www.basiccat.org/how-to-convert-traditional-comics-into-webtoon/>`_

* `Batch OCR and Translation of Images and PDF Files <https://www.basiccat.org/batch-ocr-and-translate-image-and-PDF/>`_

Webpage Images and Browser Extensions
--------------------------------

Through a browser extension, images on webpages and manga/comic reading sites are sent to ImageTrans for local translation, and the results are automatically returned to your current project. It works on both phone (iOS/Android) and desktop browsers.

* `How to Translate Images on a Webpage <https://www.basiccat.org/translate-images-on-webpages-with-extension/>`_
* `Manga Image Translation Browser Extension <https://www.basiccat.org/manga-image-translation-browser-extension/>`_
* `Translating Manga, Doujinshi and Other Images on iPhone with a Browser Extension <https://www.basiccat.org/iphone-browser-extension-doujin-manga-image-translation/>`_
* `Translating Manga Images on Android Browsers with an Extension <https://www.basiccat.org/android-browser-extension-manga-image-translation/>`_

Video and Subtitles
--------------------------------

Processing hardcoded subtitles (text burned into the video frames): extracting, translating and re-inserting them, or removing them outright. Often combined with the computer-aided audiovisual translation software Silhouette.

* `Extracting Hardcoded Subtitles from a Video <https://www.basiccat.org/how-to-extract-hardcoded-subtitle/>`_
* `How to Remove Subtitles from a Video <https://www.basiccat.org/how-to-remove-subtitles-from-a-video/>`_
